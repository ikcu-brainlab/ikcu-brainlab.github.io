About the lab

From biomedical data to intelligent healthcare

Led by Dr. Mehmet Akif Özdemir, the BRAIN Lab is an interdisciplinary laboratory at İzmir Kâtip Çelebi University, Türkiye, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, biomedical engineering, signal processing and medical imaging.

From neural activity and physiological signals to medical images, cellular responses and molecular data, we study how AI can turn complex biomedical information into meaningful, actionable knowledge, supporting early diagnosis, predictive and personalized medicine, and next-generation digital health.

Mission

To advance biomedical research and healthcare innovation through intelligent, reliable and interpretable computational technologies, bridging AI research and real-world biomedical challenges by combining advanced algorithms, experimental data, biomedical instrumentation and interdisciplinary expertise.

Vision

A future in which artificial intelligence plays a meaningful and responsible role in understanding human health, improving clinical decision-making and enabling more accessible, personalized healthcare.
