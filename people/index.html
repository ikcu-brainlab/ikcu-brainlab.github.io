Principal investigator

Mehmet Akif Özdemir, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Biomedical Engineering
Director & Principal Investigator, BRAIN Lab
Vice Dean, Faculty of Engineering and Architecture

Dr. Özdemir's research focuses on artificial intelligence in healthcare, with emphasis on biomedical signal and image processing, computational neuroscience, deep learning architectures and predictive medicine. From 2022 to 2024 he was a Visiting Research Scholar at the Berenson-Allen Center for Noninvasive Brain Stimulation, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, Harvard Medical School, where he worked on EEG biomarkers and the therapeutic efficacy of repetitive TMS in Alzheimer's disease. He also serves as Senior Associate Editor of BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making.
