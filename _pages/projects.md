    <h3>AI-Supported Modular Optical Platform for Automated Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing</h3>
    <p>Antimicrobial resistance (AMR) is a major challenge for global healthcare. Conventional susceptibility tests often need long incubation and manual evaluation, delaying clinically relevant results.</p>
    <p>This project develops an AI-supported modular optical platform that combines optical sensing, automated measurement, controlled incubation and machine learning to monitor bacterial growth and support earlier susceptibility assessment, contributing to affordable, accessible and automated microbiological diagnostics.</p>

## Emerging research directions

The BRAIN Lab is expanding into several emerging areas of biomedical artificial intelligence:

- Explainable AI-based investigation of interoceptive processes, early life adversity and alexithymia using resting-state and task-based EEG.
- AI-assisted portable electrochemiluminescence platforms for rapid bacterial identification and quantification.
- Intelligent medication dispensing systems integrating sensors, artificial intelligence and active learning.
- Multimodal learning for early prediction of spheroid quality from optical microscopy and electrochemical measurements.
- Adaptive sensor fusion for autonomous robotic systems.
- MRI segmentation and language model-based clinical decision support for automated radiology reporting.
- Real-time cardiac arrhythmia detection through hybrid deep learning and mobile healthcare applications.
