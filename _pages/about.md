    <div class="b-eyebrow">About the lab</div>
    <h2 style="margin-top:.4rem">From biomedical data to intelligent healthcare</h2>
  </div>
  <div>
    <p>Led by <a href="{{ '/people/' | relative_url }}">Dr. Mehmet Akif Özdemir</a>, the BRAIN Lab is an interdisciplinary laboratory at İzmir Kâtip Çelebi University, Türkiye, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, biomedical engineering, signal processing and medical imaging.</p>
    <p>From neural activity and physiological signals to medical images, cellular responses and molecular data, we study how AI can turn complex biomedical information into meaningful, actionable knowledge, supporting early diagnosis, predictive and personalized medicine, and next-generation digital health.</p>
  </div>
</div>
